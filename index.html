Twapia youth can do it together.

TYEI is a youth-led organization in Twapia, Zambia. We give young people the skills, mentors and leadership opportunities to change their communities.

Become a member Read our mission

Empowering Youth, Transforming Communities.

A platform for young people to grow and serve.

TYEI began in Twapia and works first with Twapia and the communities around it. As we grow, our work can reach other communities across Zambia.

Our vision

An empowered, skilled and responsible generation of young people who actively contribute to the development of Twapia and Zambia.

Our mission

To empower young people through skills development, education, mentorship, leadership opportunities, entrepreneurship and community-based initiatives that enable them to create positive and sustainable change.

Through TYEI, young people can:

  • Develop themselves
  • Gain practical and professional skills
  • Support one another
  • Serve their community
  • Build leadership abilities
  • Access opportunities
  • Create innovative solutions
  • Take part in community development
  • Contribute to Zambia's development

What we do

Ten objectives guide every TYEI program.

  • Skills development

    Practical, professional, digital, technical and entrepreneurial skills.

  • Education

    Career development, knowledge sharing and lifelong learning.

  • Leadership

    Responsible, ethical and competent young leaders.

  • Mentorship

    Connections with professionals, entrepreneurs, educators and experienced community members.

  • Entrepreneurship

    Innovation, self-employment and economic participation among young people.

  • Community development

    Projects that tackle social, educational, environmental and developmental challenges.

  • Digital empowerment

    Digital literacy, responsible technology use and awareness of online opportunities and risks.

  • Networking

    Links between young people, professionals, institutions, businesses and development organizations.

  • Volunteerism

    A culture of volunteering, service and social responsibility.

  • National development

    Encouraging young people to contribute constructively to Zambia.

What we stand for

Nine values shape how we work and how we treat each other.

Unity
Young people achieve more by working together.
Integrity
We promote honesty, transparency and accountability.
Service
Leadership is shown through service to others.
Respect
Everyone is treated with dignity, whatever their background.
Learning
We keep seeking knowledge and developing our abilities.
Innovation
We encourage creative solutions to community problems.
Responsibility
Members take responsibility for their actions and commitments.
Inclusion
We promote meaningful participation and equal opportunity.
Impact
Our activities should produce meaningful, measurable results.

Membership

Membership is open to young people who support our objectives, respect our constitution and are willing to contribute.

  • Founding members
  • General members
  • Volunteers
  • Advisors and honorary members

Who can join

  • Supports the objectives of TYEI
  • Respects the constitution
  • Shows willingness to participate
  • Conducts themselves responsibly
  • Wants to contribute to the organization and community

Member rights

  • Take part in activities
  • Share opinions respectfully
  • Propose ideas and initiatives
  • Access appropriate training
  • Volunteer for projects
  • Seek leadership positions
  • Take part in decisions where eligible

Member responsibilities

  • Respect the constitution and fellow members
  • Participate actively
  • Protect the reputation of TYEI
  • Use organizational resources responsibly
  • Promote our values
  • Avoid discrimination, harassment and abuse
  • Support community development activities
Leadership is not about being above others; it is about being willing to serve others.

Character + Commitment + Competence + Service. This is how TYEI chooses its leaders.

No one is entitled to a leadership position just because they are a founder, an early member or a friend of another leader. Executive Committee members serve two-year terms, and leaders are elected through a transparent process approved by the General Membership.

The Executive Committee

  • Executive Director / Founder
  • Deputy Executive Director
  • Secretary-General
  • Finance & Resources Officer
  • Programs & Projects Director
  • Skills & Education Coordinator
  • Youth Leadership & Mentorship Coordinator
  • Community Outreach & Development Coordinator
  • Communications & Media Coordinator
  • Partnerships & Resource Mobilization Officer

How we work

We put measurable impact first, and we work with others who share our goals.

Every major program has

A clear plan from the first idea to the final report.

  1. A clear objective
  2. Target beneficiaries
  3. Responsible persons
  4. An implementation plan
  5. A budget, where required
  6. A timeline
  7. Expected outcomes
  8. A way to measure impact
  9. A final activity report

Our partners

TYEI can partner with:

  • Community organizations
  • Schools and universities
  • Businesses
  • NGOs
  • Government institutions
  • Professional organizations
  • International development organizations
  • Other youth organizations

Every partnership rests on integrity, mutual respect, shared objectives and transparency.
