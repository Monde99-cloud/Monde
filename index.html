Empowering Youth, Transforming Communities.

A platform for young people to grow and serve.

TYEI is a youth-led, community-focused initiative. We work through education, leadership, entrepreneurship, mental health awareness, humanitarian support and community development, starting in Twapia and the communities around it, and reaching other parts of Zambia as we grow.

Our vision

An empowered, healthy, skilled, compassionate and responsible generation of young people who lead positive change and contribute to inclusive and sustainable development in Twapia and Zambia.

Our mission

To empower young people through leadership and civic engagement, mental health education, entrepreneurship and economic empowerment, education, mentorship and skills development, while mobilizing youth-led action and giving dignified support to vulnerable individuals and families.

Through TYEI, young people can:

  • Develop their leadership, knowledge and practical skills
  • Take part in community discussions and decisions
  • Promote mental health awareness and supportive communities
  • Start business ideas and reach economic opportunities
  • Support vulnerable people and families with dignity
  • Access education, mentorship and career guidance
  • Volunteer and work together on community needs
  • Create solutions to social, economic and environmental challenges

What we do

Ten objectives guide every TYEI program.

  • Youth leadership and civic engagement

    Ethical, accountable young leaders who join community dialogue, civic education, volunteering and local decision-making.

  • Mental health education and awareness

    Better understanding, less stigma and supportive environments, with links to qualified services. This is awareness, not diagnosis or treatment.

  • Entrepreneurship and economic empowerment

    Self-employment, financial literacy, business development and innovation, backed by skills and mentorship.

  • Humanitarian assistance and community welfare

    Fair, transparent and dignified support for vulnerable people and families, such as clothing, footwear, food, school supplies and hygiene items.

  • Education, mentorship and personal development

    Academic support, career guidance, mentoring, life skills and lifelong learning.

  • Community development and environmental action

    Sanitation, community clean-ups, environmental protection, tree planting and other priorities set by the community.

  • Skills and digital empowerment

    Practical, professional, vocational, technical and digital skills, with responsible technology use.

  • Networking and partnerships

    Links with professionals, entrepreneurs, schools, health and social service providers, businesses and development organizations.

  • Volunteerism and social responsibility

    A culture of compassion, service, inclusion and responsibility towards others.

  • National development

    Young people contributing constructively to Zambia's social, civic, economic and environmental development.

What we stand for

Eleven values shape how we work and how we treat each other.

Unity
Young people achieve more by working together.
Integrity
We promote honesty, transparency and accountability.
Service
Leadership is shown through service to others.
Respect
Everyone is treated with dignity, whatever their background.
Learning
We keep seeking knowledge and developing our abilities.
Innovation
We encourage creative solutions to community problems.
Responsibility
Members take responsibility for their actions and commitments.
Inclusion
We promote meaningful participation and equal opportunity.
Impact
Our activities should produce meaningful, measurable results.
Compassion
We respond to community needs with empathy and a willingness to give practical support.
Dignity

Membership

Membership is open to young people who support our objectives, respect our constitution and are willing to contribute.

  • Founding members
  • General members
  • Volunteers
  • Advisors and honorary members

Who can join

  • Supports the objectives of TYEI
  • Respects the constitution
  • Shows willingness to participate
  • Conducts themselves responsibly
  • Wants to contribute to the organization and community

Member rights

  • Take part in activities
  • Share opinions respectfully
  • Propose ideas and initiatives
  • Access appropriate training
  • Volunteer for projects
  • Seek leadership positions
  • Take part in decisions where eligible

Member responsibilities

  • Respect the constitution and fellow members
  • Participate actively
  • Protect the reputation of TYEI
  • Use organizational resources responsibly
  • Promote our values
  • Avoid discrimination, harassment and abuse
  • Support community development activities
Leadership is not about being above others; it is about being willing to serve others.

Character + Commitment + Competence + Service. This is how TYEI chooses its leaders.

No one is entitled to a leadership position just because they are a founder, an early member or a friend of another leader. Executive Committee members serve two-year terms, and leaders are elected through a transparent process approved by the General Membership.

The Executive Committee

  • Deputy Executive Director
  • Secretary-General

  • Programs & Projects Director
  • Education, Skills & Mentorship Coordinator
  • Youth Leadership & Civic Engagement Coordinator
  • Humanitarian Assistance & Community Welfare Coordinator
  • Mental Health Education & Awareness Coordinator
  • Entrepreneurship & Economic Empowerment Coordinator
  • Community Development & Environmental Action Coordinator
  • Communications & Media Coordinator
  • Partnerships & Resource Mobilization Officer

Where members or resources are limited, one coordinator may look after more than one related program for a time. Roles are separated as TYEI grows.

How we work

We put measurable impact first, protect the dignity of the people we serve, and work with others who share our goals.

Six program areas

  • Youth Leadership and Civic EngagementLeadership development, civic education, community dialogue and youth voices in local decisions.
  • Mental Health Education and AwarenessAwareness sessions, stigma reduction, well-being education and guidance to professional support.
  • Entrepreneurship and Economic EmpowermentBusiness training, financial literacy, mentorship and skills for self-employment.
  • Humanitarian Assistance and Community WelfareNeeds-based help with clothing, shoes, food, school supplies and hygiene items.
  • Education, Mentorship and Personal DevelopmentAcademic support, career guidance, mentoring and digital learning.
  • Community Development and Environmental ActionClean-ups, sanitation, tree planting and projects the community chooses.

Safeguarding, dignity and responsible delivery

Awareness, not treatment

Our mental health work is about education, awareness, stigma reduction, supportive listening and referral to qualified professionals. Members do not diagnose conditions or claim to give clinical treatment unless they are qualified and authorized.

Support with dignity

Humanitarian help follows identified needs, fairness and transparency. Donations are recorded and used for their intended purpose, without discrimination, favouritism, exploitation or any expectation of personal benefit.

Privacy and consent

Members protect the personal information of the people we support. Photos, stories and identifying details are collected or shared only with informed consent, with special care for children and other vulnerable people.
